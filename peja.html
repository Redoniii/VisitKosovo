Gateway to the mountains

Discover Peja

Peja (Pejë) lies in western Kosovo, right where the plain meets the Accursed Mountains (Bjeshët e Nemuna). Within minutes of its old bazaar you can be inside the Rugova Canyon, which makes Peja the country's favourite base for hiking, climbing and mountain trips.

The city mixes Ottoman-era streets, one of the most important medieval monasteries in the Balkans, and a relaxed café life along the Lumbardhi river — and it is the home of Peja beer.

1 Old Bazaar

Peja's long market street.

The old bazaar (Çarshia e Gjatë) has been the commercial heart of Peja since Ottoman times. Its low shopfronts are still home to craftsmen, small shops and traditional cafés.

It's the best place to start a walk through the city, try local food and pick up handmade souvenirs.

2 Bajrakli Mosque

The mosque of the bazaar.

Standing at the centre of the old bazaar, the Bajrakli Mosque was built in 1471 and is one of the most important Ottoman monuments in Peja. Its tall stone minaret is a landmark of the old town.

In its courtyard is the grave of Ali Pashë Gucia, a leader of the League of Prizren.

3 Patriarchate of Peja

Medieval frescoes at the canyon's mouth.

At the entrance to the Rugova Canyon stands the Patriarchate of Peć, a Serbian Orthodox monastery complex of several connected churches built in the 13th and 14th centuries. Its walls are covered with medieval frescoes.

The monastery is part of the UNESCO World Heritage site “Medieval Monuments in Kosovo”.

4 Rugova Canyon

Kosovo's great outdoors.

Just west of the city, the Lumbardhi river has cut a deep, winding canyon between towering limestone cliffs, reaching towards the border with Montenegro. A road follows the river up through tunnels and hairpin bends into the mountains.

Rugova is the place for hiking, via ferrata climbing, zip-lining and caving, and the long-distance Peaks of the Balkans trail passes through its villages.

5 White Drin Spring & Radavc Cave

Where the river begins.

About 11 km from Peja, near the village of Radavc, the White Drin river springs out of the mountainside and tumbles down as a waterfall.

Next to it is the Radavc Cave, with underground halls full of stalactites that can be visited on a guided tour. The shady forest and riverside restaurants make it a favourite summer escape.

Plan your visit

Good to know

  • Getting thereAbout 1½ hours by road from Prishtina, with regular buses.
  • Best forHiking, climbing and mountain trips in the Rugova valley.
  • When to goLate spring to early autumn for hiking; winter brings snow to the mountains.
  • Don't missA cold Peja beer, brewed in the city.
