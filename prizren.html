1 The Prizren Fortress

The city's watchtower.

The Prizren Fortress, also known as Kalaja, is a medieval fortress located in the city of Prizren, Kosovo. It is situated on a hill overlooking the city and was built in the 11th century during the Byzantine era. The fortress was later expanded and reinforced by the Ottoman Turks in the 16th and 17th centuries.

It has served as a military stronghold, as well as a symbol of power and prestige for various empires throughout history.

2 The Holy Saviour Church

Medieval and still standing.

The Holy Saviour Church, also known as Kisha e Shën Shpirtit, is a historic church located in Prizren, Kosovo. It was built in the 14th century during the medieval period and is considered one of the oldest and most important Christian monuments in the city.

The church is a cultural and architectural landmark and attracts tourists and religious visitors to Prizren. It is an important part of the city's rich cultural and religious heritage.

3 Sinan Pasha Mosque

The skyline of the old town.

Xhamia e Sinan Pasha is a historic mosque located in the city of Prizren, Kosovo. It was built in the 16th century during Ottoman rule and is considered one of the oldest and most important Islamic architectural monuments in the city.

The mosque is named after its patron, Sinan Pasha, who was a high-ranking Ottoman official. It is a cultural and architectural landmark and attracts tourists and religious visitors to Prizren.

4 The Stone Bridge

Centuries over the Bistrica.

The Stone Bridge, also known as Ura e Gurit, is a historic bridge located in Prizren, Kosovo. It is one of the oldest and most important architectural landmarks in the city and has been in use for centuries. The bridge is built over the Bistrica river and connects different parts of the city.

It is a cultural and historical landmark that attracts tourists and visitors to Prizren, and is considered a symbol of the city's rich history and cultural heritage.

5 The Prizren Hammam

A traditional Ottoman bathhouse.

The Prizren Hammam is a traditional bathhouse in the city of Prizren, Kosovo. It is a cultural and historical landmark and attracts tourists for its traditional Ottoman-style architecture and atmosphere.

The bathhouse is a popular destination for locals and visitors seeking to relax and experience the traditional culture of the region.

6 The League of Prizren

Where the Albanian national awakening began.

The League of Prizren was a political organization established in 1878 in the city of Prizren, Kosovo. The league aimed to unite Albanians and protect their rights and interests within the Ottoman Empire.

It is considered to be the first national political organization of Albanians and played a significant role in the Albanian national awakening movement. The League and its congresses are seen as important milestones in the history of Albanian nationalism.
